Dataset737 mask-only vertebra subregions, experimental checkpoint.
Trained on non-fractured VerSe19 vertebra masks with generated morphology labels.
The focal-outgrowth class is a candidate region, not a CT-confirmed osteophyte.

doi:10.1016/j.media.2021.102166.
